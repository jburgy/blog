FOURT.F

FOURT.F is Norman Brenner's classic mixed-radix Fast Fourier Transform. Drag the knots below to set the amplitude of each harmonic; a specialization of FOURT.F for one dimension and power-of-two lengths — plain JavaScript, no WebAssembly — transforms that frequency-domain spectrum into a 256-sample time-domain waveform. Fixing those two things at compile time strips out everything FOURT.F needs for the general case (factor-finding, the mixed-radix recurrence, a scratch array) and leaves just the classic radix-4 butterfly, which runs at least as fast as a general transliteration of FOURT.F compiled to WebAssembly would for this case. The result is traced on the oscilloscope and looped through the Web Audio API.

Each knot's Y position is split across its FFT bin and that bin's conjugate (k and N-k), so FOURT's inverse transform (ISIGN=+1) hands back a real-valued signal with no imaginary leftovers to discard.
