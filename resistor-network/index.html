Resistor Network

A random bond-percolation sample on a finite square lattice (size and bond probability p in the heading below): every bond is a 1-ohm resistor, present independently with probability p. Check Run to continuously generate a fresh random sample, reduce it one node elimination at a time to its single equivalent resistor, and drop that value into the histogram before starting over. The lone tick mark on the p slider is the lattice's exact bond-percolation threshold (pc = 1⁄2, by self-duality); the rightmost, red histogram bar counts samples that never connected the two sides at all. Node elimination follows the general star-mesh (Y-Δ) transform that D. J. Frank and C. J. Lobb specialized into a single basic operation for the square lattice in "Highly efficient algorithm for percolative transport studies in two dimensions" (Phys. Rev. B 37, 302, 1988).
